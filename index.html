Problem Statement

Liliw, Laguna is rich in historical landmarks, religious sites, festivals, footwear craftsmanship and local food. The Liliw Culture, History, Arts, and Tourism Office (CHATO) promotes these resources, coordinates tourism activities and collects tourist arrival records.

Interviews with CHATO personnel showed that tourism and cultural heritage information about Liliw is fragmented across separate channels such as Facebook, printed materials, word of mouth, vloggers and an online page. Tourist arrival records are gathered through establishment visits or photos sent through Messenger, then consolidated by hand. This process depends on staff availability and records kept in separate places, and CHATO has limited digital tools for collecting, reviewing, updating and publishing this information.

The study addresses the need for one CHATO-managed platform that brings tourism content, business submissions, visitor assistance, feedback and arrival records together.

Objectives

General Objective

To design, develop, test, and evaluate the Liliw Virtual Guide, an AI-driven Progressive Web Application for centralized cultural heritage and tourism information management for Liliw CHATO.

Specific Objectives

  1. To analyze the existing tourism and cultural heritage information management processes of Liliw CHATO and identify the challenges and system requirements.
  2. To design and develop the Liliw Virtual Guide as an AI-driven Progressive Web Application incorporating the functionalities listed under System Features.
  3. To implement role-based access and system functionalities for Admin, Editors, Local Business Owners, and Tourists/Visitors according to their designated responsibilities.
  4. To test and evaluate the functional requirements of the system through Black Box Testing to determine whether its modules and role-based functionalities operate according to their specified requirements and expected outputs.
  5. To determine the acceptability of the system among its intended users using selected Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) constructs: Perceived Ease of Use, Perceived Usefulness, Attitude Toward Use, Behavioral Intention to Use, and Actual System Use.

Methodology

Research DesignDescriptive-Developmental
Development ModelAgile
Functional TestingBlack-Box Testing
User EvaluationTechnology Acceptance Model

The descriptive component examined CHATO's existing processes, information needs and stakeholder requirements through interviews, observation and other fact-finding activities. The developmental component used those requirements to design, build, test and refine the system.

Development followed the Agile model in six iterative phases, revisited whenever testing or user feedback called for changes:

  1. Requirements PlanningTranslate CHATO processes, user roles and needs into functional and non-functional requirements.
  2. System DesignInterface, navigation, database, architecture and role permissions.
  3. Development & IntegrationBuild and connect the modules, database and external services.
  4. TestingStructured black-box test cases comparing actual against expected results.
  5. Deployment & User EvaluationIntended users try role-specific functions and complete the evaluation questionnaire.
  6. Revision & RefinementFix issues and improve the system based on test findings and feedback.

Testing and evaluation. Ten qualified IT system testers ran the prepared black-box test cases. A separate System User Evaluation with intended users (Admin, Editors, Local Business Owners and Tourists/Visitors) measured acceptance using five TAM constructs. Results were analyzed using frequency and percentage, weighted mean, Likert-scale interpretation and standard deviation.

Built with: Next.js · Supabase · Progressive Web App · deployed on Vercel

Media Gallery & Storytelling
Photos, videos and narrated stories about Liliw's culture and heritage.
Progressive Web App

Records tourist arrivals by scanning QR codes at selected locations.

Visitors rate and comment on their experience; CHATO manages the responses.
